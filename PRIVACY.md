1. Data Collection and Usage
PinPhotos operates entirely offline and does not collect, store, track, or transmit any personal data, usage metrics, or analytics. The app does not request or possess the Android INTERNET permission, making it technically impossible for it to transmit data off your device.

2. Local Storage
The app relies on Android's native Storage Access Framework to display photos from a folder you explicitly choose. The app remembers your folder selection locally on your device to function correctly. This selection data never leaves your device.

3. Third-Party Services
PinPhotos contains no advertising frameworks, third-party analytics SDKs, or background tracking software.

4. Changes to This Policy
If the data practices of this application ever change, this Privacy Policy will be updated. However, the core philosophy of PinPhotos is strict offline privacy.
